Shipping and orders

How long does delivery take?

This is a demo site, so no orders are shipped. In the story of the brand, standard delivery takes 3 to 5 working days.

Can I change my order?

Demo orders are not real and cannot be changed. You can empty your demo bag at any time.

Sizing

How do KADENRA shoes fit?

Most models fit true to size in EU sizing. Esker W is built on our women’s last; Youth models run from EU 28 to 35.

Between sizes?

Go up half a size for hiking boots, where feet swell over a long day, and choose your usual size for trail runners.

Care and repair

How do I clean suede and nubuck?

Let mud dry, brush it off with a soft brush, then refresh the nap. Avoid direct heat when drying.

Can my shoes be resoled?

Boots with a stitched or replaceable sole can be resoled. Ask at a Fieldhouse for a repair quote.

Returns

What is the returns policy?

There is nothing to return on a demo site. A real store would usually offer 30 days on unworn shoes.
