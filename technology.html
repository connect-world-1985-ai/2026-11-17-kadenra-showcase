Midsole

Cellform

An organic hex-lattice zone inside the midsole. It cushions where you land and stays stable where you push off. Visible through the heel window on Esker and Esker W, as a full layer in Moraine and a core in Kame.

Outsole

Rootgrip

Our outsole rubber, with lugs that follow the hex cell outline. Two specialist compounds: Climb for smearing on rock, and Cold for grip in low temperatures.

Membrane

Weatherline

A waterproof, breathable lining for boots that live in wet ground: Tarn Mid, Basalt WP, Combe, Cairn, Firn, Drift and more.
