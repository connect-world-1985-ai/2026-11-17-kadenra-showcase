Our story

Est. on the trail, built to last.

KADENRA began with a simple idea: shoes should be shaped by the ground they cross, not by the season they launch in.

Our name comes from cadence, the steady rhythm of steps on a long day out. Our mark is three rounded hexagons, like contour lines on a map, and like the basalt columns where nature makes its own hexagons. That shape runs through everything we make.

Every model takes its name from a landform: an esker, a tarn, a cirque, a fell. Variants get a plain suffix, never a new name. Our technologies have plain names too: Cellform, Rootgrip, Weatherline.

We use recycled mesh and knit, cork, wool, and leather from audited tanneries. We design for repair and resoling, and we would rather you wore one pair for years than three for a season.
